From row to PDF in about 15 seconds

You don't need a template or a new tool, and you don't have to copy anything. Rowvoice works with the sheet you already keep.

Open it in your sheet

Rowvoice finds your header row and matches columns like "Customer", "Total" and "Due date" for you. Starting fresh? Insert a ready-made starter sheet in one click.

Add your business details

Add your name, address, logo and payment instructions once, then choose a Classic or Modern template.

Select rows, click Create

Each row becomes a numbered PDF in your Drive. The sheet gets the invoice number, an Unpaid status and a link to the PDF.

Built for people who live in spreadsheets

Your data stays yours

It can only open the spreadsheet you use it in and the files it creates. Invoices are saved in your own Google Drive.

Multiple line items

Put one item per line in a cell, like Website audit | 450, and the total adds itself up.

Batch invoicing

Select up to 200 rows and create them all at once, with a progress bar. Rows that already have an invoice are skipped.

Numbering you can trust

Invoice numbers go up in order with no duplicates, even when two people click Create at the same moment.

Email and track payment

On Pro, send each invoice from your own Gmail with the PDF attached. Mark invoices Paid in one click. Preview any invoice before a number is used.

Tax, discounts and any currency

Add VAT, GST or sales tax, per-row discounts, notes and your tax ID. Amounts and currency follow your spreadsheet's locale.

Questions

What can Rowvoice access?

It can open only the spreadsheet you use it in and files it created itself, such as your invoice PDFs and logo. It can't browse or read the rest of your Google Drive. It can send email as you, but only when you choose to email an invoice. It can't read your inbox. See the privacy policy for details.

Where are my invoices stored?

They're saved in a "Rowvoice" folder in your own Google Drive. We don't keep copies. Your client data never touches our servers.

Do I need to change how my sheet is laid out?

Usually not. Rowvoice finds your header row, even below a title, and matches common column names. You can adjust any column in the sidebar. If you don't have a sheet yet, insert the starter sheet.

What happens when I hit the free limit?

You can still create 5 invoices each calendar month. Invoices you already made are never affected. Upgrade to Pro any time for unlimited invoices.

How do I cancel Pro?

Click "Manage billing" in the Rowvoice sidebar, or use the link in your receipt email. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for. New to Pro? You can get a full refund within 30 days of your first payment. See the terms.

Does it email invoices to clients?

Yes, on Pro. Invoices are sent from your own Gmail, with the PDF attached and replies going to your business email. You write the subject and message once. On the Free plan, each invoice is a PDF in your Drive with a link in the sheet, so you can send it however you like.
