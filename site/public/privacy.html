Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

SheetInvoice ("SheetInvoice", "we", "us") is a Google Sheets add-on that turns spreadsheet rows into PDF invoices. It is operated by [LEGAL NAME / COMPANY]. This policy explains what data the add-on and this website use, where that data goes, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Your spreadsheet data and invoices stay in your Google account. The add-on runs inside Google Apps Script, and PDFs are saved to your own Google Drive.
  • The only things that leave Google go to our license server: your Google account email (so we can tell whether you're on Pro) and a count of how many invoices you created this month.
  • We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

The license check

When the sidebar opens or you create an invoice, the add-on may send a Google-signed identity token to our license server. The token includes your Google account email and account ID. The server runs on Cloudflare Workers. It checks the token and replies "free" or "pro", along with your invoice count for the month. When you create an invoice, the add-on tells the server to add one to that count. The add-on caches the plan for up to a few hours. We don't send any spreadsheet contents, client details or invoice contents to the license server.

Payments

Stripe processes Pro subscriptions. When you check out, you give your payment details to Stripe, not to us. Stripe's privacy policy applies to them: stripe.com/privacy. From Stripe we receive and keep these details, so we know your Google account has Pro:

  • Your email address
  • Your Stripe customer and subscription IDs
  • Your subscription status and renewal date

We never receive your card number. When you click "Manage billing", our server asks Stripe for a one-time link to Stripe's billing portal for your customer ID.

Google API Services User Data Policy

SheetInvoice's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We only use Google user data to provide and improve the invoicing features you see in the add-on.
  • We do not transfer it to others except as needed to provide those features, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition.
  • We do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not let humans read it unless you ask us to (for example, for support), it is needed for security or to comply with law, or it has been aggregated and anonymized.

Service providers

  • Google runs the add-on (Apps Script) and stores your data in your account (Drive and script properties).
  • Cloudflare hosts the license server and this website. Cloudflare may process technical request data such as IP addresses and timestamps, and keeps short-term logs.
  • Stripe processes payments.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts.

Retention and deletion

  • Data in your Google account. You control it. Delete the "SheetInvoice" folder in Drive to remove PDFs and your logo. To remove stored settings, uninstall the add-on, or remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Subscription records. We keep them while your subscription is active and for as long as tax and accounting law requires after that.
  • Other requests. Email us to ask for a copy of what we hold, or for deletion where the law allows.

Security

Everything sent between the add-on, our license server and Stripe is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). The license server only accepts identity tokens signed by Google, and it only accepts payment events with a valid Stripe signature.
