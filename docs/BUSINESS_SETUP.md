# Business setup overview

Rowvoice is a product of **CAPTURES BY JC LLC**, an Oklahoma limited liability company. That name, Oklahoma governing law, the refund policy, and `support@rowvoice.com` are already on the public site:

- [rowvoice.com](https://rowvoice.com)
- [Privacy policy](https://rowvoice.com/privacy)
- [Terms](https://rowvoice.com/terms)

Pro subscriptions are billed by Stripe and tied to the Google account that upgrades inside the add-on. The license Worker never receives spreadsheet contents.

This repository does not include tax identifiers, bank details, or state filing records. Keep those with the business's own records, outside git. A local, gitignored place for operator reminders is `docs/OPERATOR_NOTES.md` (copy the example file).

Public customer terms include a 30-day money-back guarantee on the first payment and a 14-day refund window if a yearly plan renews by mistake. The wording customers see is in `site/public/terms.html`.
